Expert CV Tips & Guides

Learn how to write a CV that gets you hired in 2025

Advertisement
Ad Space 728×90
🎯

Keep it to 1-2 Pages

Recruiters spend 6 seconds scanning a CV. Keep it concise — 1 page for freshers, max 2 pages for experienced professionals.

🔍

Use ATS Keywords

Include job-relevant keywords from the job description naturally in your CV. This helps pass Applicant Tracking Systems used by most companies.

📊

Quantify Achievements

Instead of "managed a team", write "managed a team of 8 engineers, delivered project 2 weeks ahead of schedule." Numbers stand out.

📧

Professional Email Only

Use a professional email like name@gmail.com. Avoid emails like coolboy123@yahoo.com — it creates a poor first impression.

🖊️

Tailor for Each Job

Customize your CV summary and skills section for each application. A targeted CV gets 3x more callbacks than a generic one.

✅

Proofread Thoroughly

Spelling mistakes and grammatical errors are an instant rejection. Read your CV at least 3 times before sending it out.
