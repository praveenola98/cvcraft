About CVCraft Pro

We believe everyone deserves a professional CV, regardless of their budget. Built to give every job seeker the tools to present themselves professionally — for free.

Our Mission

To democratize access to professional CV creation tools. We want to eliminate the barrier between talent and opportunity by providing world-class CV templates — completely free, forever.

🎯 What We Do

CVCraft Pro is a free online CV builder with 120+ professional templates covering every industry — IT, Medical, Finance, Government, Fresher, Academic, and more. Create, customize, and download your CV as a perfect A4 PDF in minutes.

🔒 Your Privacy First

Unlike other CV builders that require sign-up and store your data, CVCraft Pro works entirely in your browser. We never see, store, or share any of your personal information. Your data is 100% yours.

No watermarks. No premium plans. No data collection. No login walls. Just an honest, powerful tool that helps you land your dream job. We keep the service free through non-intrusive advertising.
