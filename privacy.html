Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2025

CVCraft Pro ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our website at yourcvcraft.com.

1. Information We Collect

CVCraft Pro does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal information you enter into the CV builder. All data you enter (name, email, experience, etc.) stays entirely in your browser and is never sent to our servers.

We may collect anonymous usage data through Google Analytics including: pages visited, browser type, approximate location (country/city level), and time spent on site. This data does not include any personally identifiable information.

2. Cookies

We use cookies for:

  • Google Analytics — anonymous traffic analysis to improve our service
  • Google AdSense — to display relevant advertisements

You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time. This will not affect the CV builder functionality.

3. Google AdSense & Advertising

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on our website. This helps us keep the service free for all users. Google may use cookies to show personalized ads based on your interests and browsing history.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings or by visiting aboutads.info.

4. Data Security

Since we do not collect or store your CV data on our servers, there is no risk of your personal information being compromised through a data breach on our end. Your CV data exists only on your device.

5. Third-Party Links

Our site may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to read their privacy policies.

6. Children's Privacy

CVCraft Pro is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes your acceptance.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at our contact page or email support@yourcvcraft.com
